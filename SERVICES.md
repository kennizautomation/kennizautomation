# Services
**Business-focused AI and automation delivery**

## 1. AI voice receptionists
For businesses that need help responding to incoming enquiries while staff focus on appointments or service delivery.

**Possible scope:** Natural call handling, approved FAQs, lead capture, appointment assistance, call summaries, and human escalation. The business defines which questions the agent can answer and which require a person.

**What we agree:** Caller journeys, business hours, services covered, transfer behavior, booking boundaries, and the information staff need after a call.

[Explore the receptionist showcase](https://github.com/kennizautomation/ai-voice-receptionist).

## 2. Outbound AI calling
For teams following up with eligible enquiries or arranging conversations with prospects.

**Possible scope:** Initial lead conversations, relevant qualification, appointment assistance, requested transfers, and contact-record coordination.

**What we agree:** Contact eligibility, campaign purpose, calling windows, volume expectations, handoff destination, and outcome definitions.

[Explore Olivia's case study](https://github.com/kennizautomation/ai-outbound-calling-agent).

## 3. WhatsApp & website assistants
For businesses managing repetitive enquiries or appointment requests through messaging.

**Possible scope:** Approved answers, customer intake, qualification, scheduling assistance, confirmations, and escalation to staff. Channel and platform selection depends on the existing account and required experience.

[Explore the WhatsApp showcase](portfolio/whatsapp-appointment-automation.md).

## 4. n8n & Make.com business automation
For teams spending time moving the same information between apps.

**Possible scope:** Lead intake, record coordination, staff notifications, document handling, scheduled business tasks, and operational follow-up.

**What we agree:** The source of truth, responsibility for each record, exception handling, and what a successful run should achieve.

[Explore lead and CRM automation](portfolio/n8n-lead-routing.md).

## 5. CRM & calendar integration
For teams that want customer conversations to connect with sales and scheduling.

**Possible scope:** Contact coordination, pipeline-related automation, appointment creation, booking changes, and staff notifications. Availability depends on platform permissions and the approved scope.

## 6. Existing-system review & improvement
For a voice agent or automation that is already built but behaves inconsistently.

**Possible scope:** Review of conversation behavior, missing information, unreliable handoffs, scheduling failures, duplicate records, and unclear ownership. A review results in a prioritized scope of improvements rather than unsupported promises.

## What the client receives
The agreed configured solution, validation evidence, a walkthrough, and private handover material. Account ownership, subscriptions, access, maintenance, and third-party usage charges are clarified before implementation.

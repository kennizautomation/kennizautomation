## The business challenge
An AI tool provides limited value if it is disconnected from the business process or cannot reliably hand work to the right system or person.

## The experience I aim to deliver
A configured assistant supports an agreed task, works with approved information, and communicates clearly when it needs human input.

## Scope we can agree
### Use-case definition
Identify what the assistant should accomplish and where it should stop or escalate.

### Platform setup
Configure the selected tools around the business's requirements and access.

### Business integration
Connect the approved applications and keep responsibilities clear.

### Review and improvement
Validate behavior against agreed scenarios and address issues within the scope.

## A good fit for
Businesses introducing AI into a defined process or improving an existing setup.

## Relevant expertise
AI assistants, workflow automation, tool integrations, and business-process design. Product selection is specific to the engagement.

## What a client can review
Before acceptance, we agree a small set of realistic customer or staff scenarios. The review focuses on whether the system performs the required actions accurately, gives clear responses, and passes unresolved work to a person appropriately. Exact tests and internal rules remain private.

## Engagement deliverables
Requirements review, agreed solution scope, configuration of the selected tools, validation, a walkthrough, and private handover materials. Features, ownership, support, and third-party dependencies are set out in the project agreement.

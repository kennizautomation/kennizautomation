# Working together
**A clear process from first discussion to handover**

## 1. Understand the business
We identify the customer journey, the repetitive work, the people involved, and the desired business outcome. I review the tools already in place before proposing changes.

**Client contribution:** Business context, approved information, existing accounts, and a responsible project contact.

## 2. Define the scope
We agree what the system should handle, what it should hand to a person, and what is outside the first release. For voice projects, this includes calls, appointments, and transfer expectations.

**Deliverable:** A written scope with acceptance criteria, dependencies, milestones, and any required third-party services.

## 3. Plan the customer experience
We review the tone, necessary questions, customer choices, and handoff experience. The focus is a clear interaction that represents the business accurately.

**Deliverable:** An agreed experience and business rules, shared privately.

## 4. Configure and integrate
I connect the approved platforms and configure the agreed behavior in the designated accounts. Access is limited to what is needed for the engagement.

**Deliverable:** A reviewable configuration or test environment appropriate to the project.

## 5. Validate the important scenarios
We check the ordinary journey and relevant exceptions: unavailable appointments, incomplete information, unsuccessful transfers, and integration failures.

**Deliverable:** An agreed test record and a list of any remaining issues. A screenshot alone is not treated as a full execution test.

## 6. Review with the business
You review the behavior and provide feedback against the agreed scope. We address in-scope issues before acceptance.

**Deliverable:** A solution approved against the agreed acceptance criteria.

## 7. Launch and hand over
When authorized, the approved configuration is put into use. I provide a walkthrough and the operational information your team needs to manage it.

**Deliverable:** Private handover documentation and a clear owner for ongoing operations.

## 8. Support and improve
Support duration, bug-fix scope, maintenance, and later enhancements are set in the project agreement. Changes in business rules or third-party platforms may require new work.

## Timelines
A focused first release may fit a 7–10 day implementation window after access and requirements are ready. This is a planning estimate, not a universal promise. Larger integrations, approvals, migrations, and testing needs affect the schedule. A specific milestone plan follows discovery.

## Ownership and running costs
We agree which accounts own the configuration and what access each person needs. Platform subscriptions, calling, messaging, and model usage are identified separately from the implementation scope. Client earnings and contract amounts are not published in this portfolio.

## Communication
The project agreement defines update cadence, review points, and the channel used for decisions. Upwork enquiries can remain in Upwork Messages.

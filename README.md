## Solution Areas

### 📞 AI Receptionist + Appointment Booking
A caller reaches a voice agent, explains what they need, checks actual calendar availability, confirms an appointment, and has the outcome recorded in a CRM.

### 💬 WhatsApp Lead Qualification
A chatbot responds to incoming inquiries, asks relevant questions one at a time, identifies customer needs, and routes qualified leads to the right team or booking flow.

### 🔄 Automated Lead & CRM Workflows
An incoming form, call, or message triggers a workflow that validates information, checks for duplicates, updates the CRM, and notifies the assigned person.

### 📅 Scheduling and Follow-Up
Connected automations handle booking, rescheduling, reminders, callback requests, and status updates while accounting for unavailable time slots and integration failures.

## My Approach

1. **Understand the process** — Identify the business goal, customer journey, and edge cases.
2. **Design the workflow** — Map conversation logic, integrations, and human handoffs.
3. **Build and connect** — Configure agents, automations, APIs, and business systems.
4. **Test real scenarios** — Check successful paths, failures, transfers, and fallback behavior.
5. **Document and hand over** — Make the solution understandable and maintainable.

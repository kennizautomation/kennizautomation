## The business challenge
Sales teams need to understand which enquiries fit their service and what information is still missing. Unstructured notes can make follow-up inconsistent.

## The experience I aim to deliver
A prospect's stated needs, timing, and fit can be summarized clearly so the team can choose an appropriate next action. Missing information should remain visible rather than being guessed.

## Scope we can agree
### Qualification design
Define the information the business needs to understand fit and readiness.

### Conversational intake
Collect relevant answers through the agreed voice, chat, or form experience.

### Prioritization support
Present information consistently so staff can focus their follow-up.

### Sales handoff
Provide a concise summary of the enquiry, the next step, and any unanswered questions.

## A good fit for
Teams receiving a mix of relevant enquiries, incomplete requests, and leads needing further discussion.

## Relevant expertise
Conversation design, CRM coordination, and business automation. No scoring formula, code, or deployable rule set is published.

## What a client can review
Before acceptance, we agree a small set of realistic customer or staff scenarios. The review focuses on whether the system performs the required actions accurately, gives clear responses, and passes unresolved work to a person appropriately. Exact tests and internal rules remain private.

## Engagement deliverables
Requirements review, agreed solution scope, configuration of the selected tools, validation, a walkthrough, and private handover materials. Features, ownership, support, and third-party dependencies are set out in the project agreement.

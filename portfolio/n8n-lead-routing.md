## The business challenge
When enquiries arrive through forms, calls, chat, and spreadsheets, staff may spend more time organizing information than responding to prospects. Inconsistent ownership can lead to delays and duplicate effort.

## The experience I aim to deliver
A team can see a clear record of the enquiry, understand who should act, and use its CRM without repeatedly copying the same details between tools.

## Scope we can agree
### Lead organization
Bring approved enquiry sources into a consistent business process with useful context for the team.

### Ownership and routing
Support assignment according to the business's agreed responsibilities, service lines, or regions.

### CRM coordination
Keep relevant contact and follow-up information aligned with the chosen source of truth.

### Notifications and exceptions
Provide useful updates and make unresolved cases visible to the responsible person.

## A good fit for
Sales and service teams handling multiple enquiry sources or repeating manual CRM administration.

## Relevant expertise
n8n, Make.com, GoHighLevel, HubSpot, Zoho, Airtable, and Google Sheets. Platforms and scope are agreed around available access.

## What a client can review
Before acceptance, we agree a small set of realistic customer or staff scenarios. The review focuses on whether the system performs the required actions accurately, gives clear responses, and passes unresolved work to a person appropriately. Exact tests and internal rules remain private.

## Engagement deliverables
Requirements review, agreed solution scope, configuration of the selected tools, validation, a walkthrough, and private handover materials. Features, ownership, support, and third-party dependencies are set out in the project agreement.

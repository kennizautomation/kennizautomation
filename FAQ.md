# Questions clients often ask

### Can you work with my existing CRM and calendar?
I review the platforms, account permissions, and required behavior first. Where supported, the solution can use your existing tools instead of requiring a full replacement.

### Can you improve an agent I already have?
Yes, review and improvement can be scoped separately from a new build. Share the desired behavior and examples of the current problem privately.

### Can I hire you for one part of the project?
Yes. A defined integration, booking flow, conversation review, or automation improvement can be scoped as an individual engagement.

### Will this replace every human interaction?
The scope should define which tasks are suitable for automation and when a person takes over. Complex requests and exceptions may need staff judgment.

### What will my team need to maintain?
That depends on the platforms and configuration. The handover explains the agreed operational responsibilities and which changes require technical support.

### Are accounts and implementation files handed over?
Ownership, access, and deliverables are agreed in the engagement. Implementation material is shared privately with authorized clients, not published as a downloadable template.

### Are the screenshots real?
The Olivia gallery contains owner-supplied platform screenshots with private details concealed. They show configuration views, not independently verified performance benchmarks. Other service pages are labeled as showcases or concepts.

### Can I try a public live agent?
No public test number or live demo is offered by these repositories. Ask about an appropriate demonstration for your use case through Upwork.

### Do you guarantee a specific conversion rate?
No. Results depend on lead quality, customer needs, business processes, and the implementation. We agree what behavior will be tested and how success will be assessed.

### How do I check your reviews?
Use the [Upwork profile](https://www.upwork.com/freelancers/kenniz) and [reference page](CLIENT-REFERENCES.md). The reference page distinguishes dated profile observations, client feedback, and service listings.

### Why are prompts and source code not public?
This is a professional services portfolio. It explains the business value and presents selected evidence while keeping implementation assets private.

## The business challenge
Teams often maintain important business processes through spreadsheets, email, and documents. Repeated copying and inconsistent updates make those processes harder to manage.

## The experience I aim to deliver
Staff can spend less time moving routine information and more time reviewing meaningful exceptions, with clearer records in the tools they already use.

## Scope we can agree
### Spreadsheet operations
Automate agreed updates, checks, and routine organization of business records.

### Forms and intake
Support structured collection of information and the appropriate follow-up.

### Documents and email coordination
Connect approved administrative steps so staff do not repeat the same work across apps.

### Review and handover
Make the process understandable for the team, including what needs manual attention.

## A good fit for
Small businesses and operations teams using Google Workspace for everyday administration.

## Relevant expertise
Google Sheets, Docs, Forms, Gmail, Apps Script, and workflow integrations, selected according to the task.

## What a client can review
Before acceptance, we agree a small set of realistic customer or staff scenarios. The review focuses on whether the system performs the required actions accurately, gives clear responses, and passes unresolved work to a person appropriately. Exact tests and internal rules remain private.

## Engagement deliverables
Requirements review, agreed solution scope, configuration of the selected tools, validation, a walkthrough, and private handover materials. Features, ownership, support, and third-party dependencies are set out in the project agreement.

# Tell me about your project
Send the information you already know; a complete technical specification is not required.

## Business and goal
- What does the business do?
- What customer or staff task needs improvement?
- What would a useful first release achieve?

## Existing tools
- Which CRM, calendar, messaging, or voice platforms do you use?
- Is there an existing agent or workflow to improve?
- Which system should hold the final customer record?

## Voice projects
- Inbound, outbound, or both?
- What should the conversation accomplish?
- Should the agent book appointments, transfer calls, or capture a follow-up request?
- What operating hours, languages, and approximate call volumes should be considered?

## Chat and automation projects
- Where do enquiries or records arrive?
- What should the customer or staff member receive?
- Which tasks need human approval?
- What tends to go wrong in the current process?

## Delivery
- Preferred start date and any firm deadline.
- Who will review the solution?
- Whether staff need training or ongoing support.
- Any restrictions on client information or account access.

Do not send passwords, API keys, private customer exports, or sensitive call recordings in a public GitHub issue. Arrange access privately through the agreed project channel.

## The business challenge
Messaging enquiries often arrive while staff are serving customers. Repeated questions and manual booking coordination can make responses inconsistent and leave the team with fragmented information.

## The experience I aim to deliver
A customer can ask an approved business question, explain what they need, and move toward an appointment or human response without repeating their information unnecessarily.

## Scope we can agree
### Enquiry handling
Answers based on approved services, business information, and the limits of the assistant's role.

### Customer intake
Relevant information gathered conversationally, with a clear handoff to staff where the request needs judgment.

### Appointment assistance
Calendar coordination, customer confirmation, and booking-related notifications as supported by the selected platform.

### Staff follow-up
A useful summary or next action so a staff member can continue the conversation with context.

## A good fit for
Appointment-led service businesses, studios, salons, and teams that receive regular enquiries through WhatsApp.

## Relevant expertise
WhatsApp Cloud API, UChat, ManyChat, n8n, Make.com, and CRM/calendar integrations. The actual combination is chosen for the engagement; no one deployed stack is claimed here.

## What a client can review
Before acceptance, we agree a small set of realistic customer or staff scenarios. The review focuses on whether the system performs the required actions accurately, gives clear responses, and passes unresolved work to a person appropriately. Exact tests and internal rules remain private.

## Engagement deliverables
Requirements review, agreed solution scope, configuration of the selected tools, validation, a walkthrough, and private handover materials. Features, ownership, support, and third-party dependencies are set out in the project agreement.

## Evidence and status
This is a service showcase, not a claim of a specific completed contract or measured client outcome. A real deployment case study can be discussed when relevant evidence is available and sharing is authorized.
